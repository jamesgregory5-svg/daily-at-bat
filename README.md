# Daily At-Bat ⚾

A daily baseball hitting game. Every day you get 9 at-bats against the same 9 pitchers as everyone else, in a new themed ballpark.

**Play:** https://jamesgregory5-svg.github.io/daily-at-bat/

- Each pitcher has a name, a throwing hand, and a pitch mix: 4-seam fastball, sinker, cutter, slider, curveball, changeup, or splitter, from about 70 to 99 mph.
- Real counts: balls, strikes, walks, strikeouts, and fouls. A foul with two strikes doesn't strike you out.
- Tap **SWING**, tap the field, or press **space** when the marker hits the sweet spot. Early swings get pulled, late swings go the other way.
- The color of the pitch trail tells you the pitch type. You can turn on manual aim with a contact circle in settings (mouse, arrow keys/WASD, or drag).
- Box score with AB, H, 2B, 3B, HR, BB, K, runs (from simple base running), and total bases, plus a daily score and an emoji result you can share.
- You get one ranked run per day. Your progress saves after every pitch, so you can leave and come back. The game also tracks your streak and best score.
- Practice mode lets you pick the pitch type, speed, location, and pitcher hand, with an optional pitch-name hint. "New lineup" plays a random set of 9 pitchers that doesn't count.

Everything is in one `index.html` with no outside requests. You can download it and open it as a local file.
